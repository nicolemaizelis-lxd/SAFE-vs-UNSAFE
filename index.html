Adult settings

Narration is always enabled. Tap Start with sound to activate it. Keep your device volume up. Pictures stay still while the action is spoken. Answers appear after the action, and each button is highlighted as its name is spoken. There is no time limit. An incorrect answer prompts “Try again” and repeats the scene. The student must choose correctly before moving on.

Uses your browser’s speech voice. A supported English voice is required; some voices need internet. If speech fails, the game pauses and asks for help instead of continuing silently. Voice quality depends on the device.

How the card topics are used: Calmly saying “not ready” is safe. Following directions refers to walking around a spill. Food sharing is shown without an adult’s allergy check. Yelling is directed into someone’s face; shouting for help in danger is different. Words, cards and gestures are all valid ways to ask for help.
